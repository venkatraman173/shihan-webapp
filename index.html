About Shihan

Titled Shihan — "Master of the Masters" — Pradeep Babu has been teaching karate since 2011 and has trained students between the ages of 2.5 and 86 years. He holds a B.Sc. (Mathematics) from Loyola College, Chennai and is pursuing M.A. Public Administration from the University of Madras.

Beyond the dojo, he is a Rashtrapati (President) Scout Awardee, a 2nd Degree Black Belt in Taekwondo (Kukkiwon), and a founding member of Ezhuvaai Ondraga — a social group serving underprivileged children and elders.
